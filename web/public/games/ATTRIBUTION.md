Durak Online sticker images sourced from public packs https://tgtg.su/pack/DurakOnlineAllEmoji (26) and https://botobot.ru/catalog/stickers/yodurakonline (28). Original Durak Online artwork; no open license is asserted. Local source URLs and SHA-256 checksums in durak-stickers.json. The packs share 25 drawings: visually identical renditions are deduplicated to 29 local images, preferring full-size Botobot images. All 54 protocol IDs map to canonical assets in the manifest. Source hashes describe original fetched files; assetSha256 describes the retained rendition.
